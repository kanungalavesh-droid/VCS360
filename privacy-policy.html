Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Vardhman Consultancy Services ("VCS", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Policy explains what information we collect through vcs360.com, why we collect it, and how it's used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly — when you submit our consultation/contact form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, city, the service you're interested in, and any message you include.

Information collected automatically — like most websites, we use analytics and advertising tools that automatically collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and how you interact with the site, via cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your consultation request and provide the services you've asked about
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding your enquiry
  • To understand site traffic and improve our content and services
  • To measure and improve our advertising (e.g. Google Ads, Meta/Facebook Ads)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use the following third-party tools, which may set cookies or similar technologies in your browser:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — to understand site traffic and visitor behaviour
  • Meta Pixel — to measure the effectiveness of our Facebook/Instagram advertising
  • Google Ads — to measure the effectiveness of our search advertising

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so may affect some functionality of the site.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate this website, and your information may pass through them:

  • Google Firebase — securely stores consultation form submissions
  • Netlify — hosts this website
  • Google (Gemini AI, Analytics, Ads) — content assistance and analytics
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising
  • Unsplash — royalty-free stock photography used on our blog

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle data; we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

Consultation form submissions are stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), protected by access controls that restrict who can view or manage this data. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain consultation enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and maintain business records, or until you request deletion.
