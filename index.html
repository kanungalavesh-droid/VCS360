Common Questions

Things People Ask Before They Start

Is the initial consultation really free?
Yes, completely. Your first discussion with our advisor covers your situation, goals, and options — at no charge and with no obligation to proceed further. There are no hidden fees in this stage.
How quickly will someone get back to me?
We guarantee a response within 2 business hours of receiving your request. For urgent matters, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach our team directly.
Do you help with both personal and business finance?
Absolutely. VCS serves individuals (salaried, professionals, HNIs, NRIs) and businesses (startups, SMEs, established companies) across all financial domains — from personal ITR filing to full-scale business advisory and funding.
Is my financial information kept confidential?
Without exception. We treat all client data and discussions with strict confidentiality. Your financial details are never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.
Can I get assistance if I'm not in the same city?
Yes. We serve clients pan-India and internationally through video calls, phone consultations, email, and WhatsApp. Distance is not a barrier to receiving expert guidance from VCS.
What makes VCS different from other financial advisors?
Most advisors specialize in one area. VCS provides integrated expertise across taxation, compliance, investments, loans, insurance, and business advisory — enabling holistic financial planning from a single trusted partner, not a patchwork of specialists.
